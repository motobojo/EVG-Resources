Fringe uses of EVG controls

Last updated: 2026-08-28    maintained by Tom Morrissey (motobojo@outlook.com)

Currently the User Guide for the EchoVision Glasses describes the mainstream uses of the controls of the glasses.  The controls of interest are the Camera/Power button on the top of the left arm, the AI/Action button on the top of the right arm, and the touchpad on the front outside of the left arm.  I was curious about the currently undocumented interactions of these controls.  That is what is explored here.  I don’t know the degree to which these interactions are considered features or whether the behaviors are just a consequence of the current implementation.  I’ll leave that to AGIGA to sort out.  Given this undocumented state of these behaviors, the reader should not consider these uses “supported” by AGIGA.  Use them at your own risk and be aware the behavior could change at any time.

The glasses are always in a certain state.  After you power up the glasses and get the confirmation that the glasses are ready you are in the sate I’ll refer to as “idle”.  Many of the mainstream, currently defined behaviors are assuming you are in this “idle” state when you invoke the control.  For example, a single tap of the AI button takes you out of the “idle” state and places you in the sate of Q&A mode (or scene description).    The presentation that follows is organized around the starting state and then how each control behaves when invoked from that state.

Q&A / Scene Description state

This state allows for voice interaction with the AI, but that voice interaction is not required.  After initiating this  via a single press of the AI/Action button, you will hear the sound of two tones rising in pitchthat indicates you have entered Q&A mode and if you wish to prompt the AI via voice, this is the time to provide that prompt as the glasses are listening for that prompt.   After a few seconds of hearing nothing the glasses will proceed to analyze the scene and provide you with a scene description.  After the AI response is provided you will hear the tone indicating the glasses are listening for a follow-up prompt.  If no prompt is provided in a few seconds Q&A mode will be terminated and you will hear the double doink sound indicating this.
A transcript of the AI interaction will be saved to the history.

2 second press of the AI button

This is a defined, supported operation.  It completes and terminates the current AI operation and takes the user back to the idle state.  This is considered the graceful way to indicate you are done with this AI mode.  You will hear a double doink tone indicating the termination of this AI mode.  The  AI dialogue you just completed will be saved in the History.

Single press of the AI button

This terminates the current Q&A mode and restarts a new Q&A mode session.  This is equivalent to doing the 2 second press of the AI button and then doing a single press of the AI button.  The only difference is that you will not hear a double doink sound indicating the end of the current Q&A session.  You will just hear the tone indicating the glasses are listening for a prompt in the new Q&A session.

Double press of the AI button

This terminates the current Q&A mode and starts a new Live AI mode session.  This is equivalent to doing the 2 second press of the AI button and then doing a double press of the AI button. The only difference is that you will not hear a double doink sound indicating the end of the current Q&A session.  You will just hear the message indicating the start of the Live AI session.

Triple press of the AI button

This terminates the current Q&A mode and starts a new Reading mode session.  This is equivalent to doing the 2 second press of the AI button and then doing a triple press of the AI button. The only difference is that you will not hear a double doink sound indicating the end of the current Q&A session.  You will just hear the message indicating the start of the Reading mode session.

Quadruple press of the AI button (experimental)

This terminates the current Q&A mode and starts a new Quick Text session.  This is equivalent to doing the 2 second press of the AI button and then doing a quadruple press of the AI button. The only difference is that you will not hear a double doink sound indicating the end of the current Q&A session.  You will just hear the AI start right into Quick Text reading when it finds some text to read.

5x press of the AI button

This terminates the current Q&A mode and starts a new Send Feedback session.  This is equivalent to doing the 2 second press of the AI button and then doing a 5x press of the AI button. The only difference is that you will not hear a double doink sound indicating the end of the current Q&A session.  You will just hear the Send Feedback session starting.

6x press of the AI button

This does NOT terminate the Q&A mode.  It does announce the start of the Bluetooth pairing. If the AI is in the process of describing the scene or replying to the Q&A prompt you will hear the Bluetooth pairing announcement intermixed with the AI voicing.  So, basically launches the BT pairing process running simultaneously with the ongoing Q&A operation.

30 second press of the AI button

In this context, after a couple of seconds of the press is interpreted as the AI operation termination.  You can’t get to the Full factory reset directly from here.

Single press of the camera button

This does NOT terminate the Q&A mode.  It takes the picture and makes the camera shutter sound.  That sound will be intermixed with any AI voicing being done in the Q&A mode.  So, the picture is taken while the Q&A mode continues.

Double press of the camera button

This does NOT terminate the Q&A mode.  The behavior here is quite inconsistent.  Sometimes this initiates the recording of a vdeo.  Sometimes it announces that fact.  Other times it does not and may or may not actually be recording the video. If it is recording the video you can stop the video recording with the single press of the Camera button.  If it was not recording a video that action might take a picture.  In all cases though, the Q&A mode continues right along.  I would consider this to be a less than graceful interaction.

Triple press of the camera button

This does not immediately terminate the Q&A mode, but eventually does. It does launch the Status Report.  The status report is voiced intermixed to some degree with the voicing that may be occurring from the Q&A action.  After completing the status report voicing the Q&A voicing is truncated and Q&A mode is terminated.  I would consider this to be a less than graceful interaction.

Announce the status of the glasses.

Press Camera/Power button for 5 seconds

This pretty much does what you would expect.  It interrupts Q&A mode and powers off the glasses.  As best I can tell if you had a Q&A dialogue in process that dialogue is saved in the History.

Press camera button for 30 seconds

Force a restart of the glasses.

Maybe don’t try this.  At 5 seconds of having the power button pressed it gave the announcement for powering off the glasses.  I kept pressing to get the restart operation.  At about 20 seconds it said sorry and that was all she wrote.  The glasses were off and would not restart.  When I initiated this my glasses SOC was 25% so maybe this was a low power oddity.  I plugged the glasses in for USB-C charging.  After a short while (a few minutes) I successfully powered up the glasses while they were still charging.  It worked.  They weren’t toast, thankfully.

Touchpad hold

Terminate this AI interaction (Q&A mode).  This is mainstream operation.
